How to find and cancel the subscriptions you forgot about

Nov 30, 2026 · 6 minute read · Filed under Deals

I ran this check on our own accounts and found charges I'd stopped thinking about months ago. A free trial that turned into a paid plan. An app I used once. A streaming service we watched for one show.

None of them were big. Together they added up to real money every month. Here's the exact check I use, start to finish, in about 30 minutes.

Step 1: Pull three months of statements

One month isn't enough. Some subscriptions bill every three months or once a year, and those are the ones that sneak past you. Grab the last three months from:

  • Every bank account you use
  • Every credit card, including the store card in the junk drawer
  • PayPal, Apple, Google Play and Amazon, since they bill on their own

Step 2: Highlight anything that repeats

Go line by line and mark every charge that shows up more than once, even if the amount changes a little. Watch for odd names. A lot of companies bill under a parent company name you won't recognize at first.

Quick test for a strange charge

Search the exact name on your statement plus the word "charge." Someone else has almost always asked what it is.

Step 3: Sort each one into keep, cancel or downgrade

Ask one question for each: "Did I use this in the last 30 days?" If the answer is no, it goes on the cancel list. If you use it but not much, check for a cheaper plan or an ad-supported tier.

Step 4: Cancel the right way

  • Cancel inside the account settings, not by deleting the app. Deleting the app does not stop the bill.
  • Take a screenshot of the cancellation confirmation.
  • For anything billed through your phone, cancel in the App Store or Google Play subscriptions page.
  • Set a reminder to check your next statement and make sure the charge stopped.

Step 5: Stop it from happening again

When you start a free trial, put the end date in your phone calendar the same minute you sign up. That one habit catches most of these before they ever charge you.
