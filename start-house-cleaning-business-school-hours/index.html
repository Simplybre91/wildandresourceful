How to start a house cleaning business during school hours

Nov 24, 2026 · 8 minute read · Filed under Trades

You don't need a free week to start a cleaning business. You need the hours between school drop-off and pickup, one honest price, and a first client who tells a friend.

Start with the schedule you actually have. If drop-off is 8:00 and pickup is 3:00, your work window is about 8:30 to 2:30 once you count driving. That's one bigger clean or two small ones a day, and that's enough to start. Quote from that real window, not from the day you wish you had.

Price the first job without guessing

Walk the house once, count the rooms that get cleaned every visit, and time yourself honestly on your own house first. A simple worked example:

A 3-bedroom, 2-bath home, cleaned every two weeks

Your timed pace says about 2.5 hours, inside the school window.

Quoted flat at $145: a number you can say out loud without flinching.

The tool I build my bids with lives on my Gumroad page, linked from the icons at the top of this site.

Book the second client before the first clean

Ask the first client one question at booking: "Who on your street hates cleaning?" One name is worth more than any ad. Send that person one short text the same day, while your name is still fresh.

Your one next stepTime yourself cleaning one bathroom and one kitchen at home. That pace is the start of every honest quote you'll ever give.
